# Meridian | User guide
Demonstration prototype for programme managers and PMOs. Use fictional data only. Public mode uses deterministic processing and session-isolated review histories; reviewer identities are simulated.

## Operating procedure
1. Open the portfolio website and select Launch Live SteerCo UI.
2. Review executive health, costs, milestones and risks in tab 1.
3. In tab 2 review the five-role outputs and section-level human review gates.
4. In tab 3 reconcile finance and challenge questionable records.
5. In tab 4 review milestones, changes and backlog.
6. In tab 5 review RAID, benefits and source conflicts.
7. In tab 6 resolve exceptions with evidence and confirmation, review audit history and download eligible reports and rollover.

## Try it
Reject the November 2026 actual expenditure of EUR 295,000 in tab 3. Enter a reconciliation reason, Finance Team owner and due date. Verify exports are held in tab 6. Attempt closure without evidence: it should fail. Then enter simulated evidence and reviewer Finance Controller, confirm verification and close. Check the history and eligible downloads.

## Reporting
Up to nine artefacts: monthly status report, SteerCo deck, RAID register, cost analysis, benefits analysis, backlog review, monthly change summary, five-role JSON outputs and next-month workbook.

## Why it matters
PMs can focus on decisions and accountability rather than assembling reports. PMOs can standardise review gates, trace exceptions and reduce the risk of reporting disputed figures. These are intended benefits, not measured savings. This is not a production system.
